General Entry Information

  • Entry Time: 4:00 PM
  • Age Restriction: Entry is restricted to individuals aged 25 years and above.

Identification

  • Identification: Attendees must present a valid photo ID at the entrance, which includes:
    • A physical student ID (if applicable).
    • A government-issued ID (e.g., passport, driver’s license, Aadhaar).
  • Vehicle Restrictions: Vehicles are strictly prohibited on campus premises.

Conduct Guidelines

  • Behavior: Attendees must maintain respectful behavior towards participants, staff, and property. Any form of harassment, misconduct, or inappropriate behavior will result in immediate removal and may involve law enforcement authorities.
  • Prohibited Substances: The possession or consumption of alcohol and illegal substances is strictly forbidden.

Safety and Security

  • Bag Checks: Only sling bags with dimensions not exceeding 25x25 cm will be permitted into the venue. All bags are subject to thorough inspection by security personnel.
  • Emergency Procedures: Attendees must familiarize themselves with the emergency exits and procedures. Follow all instructions given by security personnel during emergencies.
  • Lost Items: The organizing committee will not be held responsible for the loss of personal belongings.

Health Guidelines

  • First Aid: A first aid station will be available on-site. If medical assistance is needed, attendees should contact a staff member or security personnel immediately.

Prohibited Items

The following items are strictly prohibited within the venue:

  • Sharp objects
  • Food items and beverages
  • Toxic substances
  • Flammable items
  • Explosive materials

Special Arena for Non-GITAM Participants

A dedicated special arena will be provided inside the venue exclusively for non-GITAM participants.
